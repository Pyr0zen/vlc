# Settings frames (W6.1, W6.2, W6.3, W8.1)

Frames of the designer's video at 2 per second, 1280 px wide, for the Settings work: the
settings walkthrough, her categories, Advanced Options, the info icons, the Keyboard Shortcuts
dialog and the Customize Player screen. The Add Subtitles dialog (W6.5) is not included.

- Name: `t_MM-SS.d.jpg` = video time minutes, seconds, tenths (0 or 5); accurate to about 0.25 s.
- Segments: the time ranges of every row in redesign/BUILD_PLAN.md section 8 with work package
  W6.1, W6.2, W6.3 or W8.1, plus every redesign/VIDEO_INDEX.md row with area "settings", merged;
  plus the context between them (15:47-15:57 survey charts before the Keyboard Shortcuts dialog,
  18:55-19:11 the old Toolbars Editor the Customize Player screen replaces).
  - 6:48-6:52 (10 frames): keyboard shortcut mention (subtitle delay keys, Decision 22)
  - 7:29-7:40 (24 frames): the problem: exact font sizes hidden in the old Advanced Preferences
  - 15:33-19:28 (472 frames): the settings walkthrough: old preferences, survey, Keyboard
    Shortcuts dialog (15:57-16:03), the new Settings dialog and its categories (17:33-18:30),
    info icons and Advanced Options (18:45-18:55), old Toolbars Editor and Customize Player
    (18:56-19:28)
- Read them with the transcript (`../vlc_redesign.en.srt`) at the same times: many frames show the
  old VLC preferences or intermediate versions before her final one, and the video is edited
  (dissolves, camera zooms). The look comes from the Figma final UIs (`../figma/`, and
  `figma/figma_file.json` for exact values); Tony's decisions in CLAUDE.md win over both
  (Decision 32: dialogs open and switch instantly).
